FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Minimal Notes really free?

Yes. Downloading and using Minimal Notes costs nothing, and none of the writing features are locked behind a paid tier.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There’s no sign-up and no login. Open the app and your first note is one tap away.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, running iOS 16.0 or later.

Where are my notes stored?

On your iPhone, in a local database that is encrypted on disk. Note content is not uploaded to a server.

Can I color and bold my text?

Yes. The editing toolbar carries twelve standard colors plus bold, and it remembers the color you used last.

Is my language supported?

Minimal Notes ships in 30 languages. It can follow your system language, or you can pick one yourself in Settings.
